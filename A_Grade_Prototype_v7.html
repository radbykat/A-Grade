Parent guide

Creating a calm homework routine

A gentle routine helps children understand what to expect without making learning feel like a race.

Try these three steps

1. Prepare the space. Keep materials nearby and reduce distractions.

2. Make the plan visible. Use a simple checklist or picture schedule.

3. Build in breaks. Short focused sessions with movement breaks can help.
